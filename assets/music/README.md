# Your music

Tracks saved here play by name, no code (.ogg is best; .mp3 or .wav work too):

- `menu.ogg`: the main menu. More than one (`menu_1.ogg`, `menu_2.ogg`...) play one after
  another in a shuffled order; a single track loops.
- `<map>.ogg`: that map's matches, e.g. `highseas.ogg` (also its Storm, Hurricane and Golden
  Hour variants), `pump_house.ogg`, `haunted_yard.ogg`, `prime.ogg`.
- `match.ogg`: any match without a track of its own.

Players set the volume, or turn music off, in *Options → Music*; *Match music* turns it off
during matches only. More: [docs/SOUNDS.md](../../docs/SOUNDS.md).
