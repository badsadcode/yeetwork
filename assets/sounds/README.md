# Your own sounds

A recording saved here under a sound's name replaces the game's made-up sound:
`boom.ogg` replaces explosions; `boom_1.ogg`, `boom_2.ogg`... are variants picked at random.
The Yeets' voices are `voice_hurt`, `voice_die`, `voice_scream`, `voice_yeet`... (several takes:
`voice_hurt_1.ogg`, `voice_hurt_2.ogg`). .ogg, .wav or .mp3.

Every sound's name, the voices and recording tips: [docs/SOUNDS.md](../../docs/SOUNDS.md).
