# SpawnPoint

*The map: a Map Kit piece (Add Child Node > SpawnPoint). Script: `scripts/mapkit/spawn_point.gd`.* [All the pieces](README.md)

Where players appear. "Any" spawns are used in free-for-all; team spawns in team modes (they fall back to any spawn if a team has none). Place at floor level; facing is chosen toward the map center.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Team** | `Any` | Who starts here: Any (free-for-all), Red or Blue. Want 6 or more Any, 4 or more per team. Choices: `Any`, `Red`, `Blue`. |
