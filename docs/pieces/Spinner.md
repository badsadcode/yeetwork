# Spinner

*Building: a Map Kit piece (Add Child Node > Spinner). Script: `scripts/mapkit/spinner.gd`.* [All the pieces](README.md)

Slowly turns its children around its local Y axis in game (decorations: Prime's orb ring, Facing's crystal). Visual only: put no collision under it.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | `0.4` | How fast it turns its children about its Y, radians a second (negative: the other way). |
