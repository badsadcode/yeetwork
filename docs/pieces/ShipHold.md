# ShipHold

*Water and ships: a Map Kit piece (Add Child Node > ShipHold). Script: `scripts/mapkit/ship_hold.gd`.* [All the pieces](README.md)

Below deck on a Vessel (an engine room, a cargo hold): a box (Size, standing on this node, lined up with the ship) the sea stays out of however far she heels, and where bots knocked down a hatch find their way out: up the Ladders whose foot is in it. Put it under the Vessel; one a ship.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Size** | `(12, 6, 10)` | The hold's box, metres (standing on the node): the sea stays out of it however far she heels. |
