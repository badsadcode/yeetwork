# GrapplePoint

*Building: a Map Kit piece (Add Child Node > GrapplePoint). Script: `scripts/mapkit/grapple_point.gd`.* [All the pieces](README.md)

A ring to grapple onto. On maps whose MapSettings Grapple is "grapple points" the hook only takes hold on these (and on props and friends); aiming near one is enough. Hang them from ceilings, beams and ledge edges.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Model** | (none) | Your own model for it (a scene: an imported .glb...), instead of the built-in look and the map theme's. |
