# FlagStand

*Modes: a Map Kit piece (Add Child Node > FlagStand). Script: `scripts/mapkit/flag_stand.gd`.* [All the pieces](README.md)

Team flag base for Capture the Flag (needs one Red and one Blue). Place at floor level. Model: your own stand in place of the glowing pedestal (the flag sits 0.4 m up; the team's light stays), or the map theme's flag_stand.

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Team** | `Red` | Whose flag it holds. Capture the Flag needs one of each. Choices: `Red`, `Blue`. |
| **Model** | (none) | Your own stand (the flag sits 0.4 m up; the team light stays). |
